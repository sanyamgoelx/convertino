Convert any file from a radial wheel: **hold Alt and right-click a file** in File Explorer (on a Mac: hold Option and right-click in Finder), then pick a format. That's the default and needs no setup. Prefer the keyboard? A shortcut is also available, changeable in Settings › General.

**Which file do I download?**
- Windows: `Convertino_…_x64-setup.exe`
- Mac with Apple silicon (M1 and later): `Convertino_…_aarch64.dmg`
- Mac with Intel: `Convertino_…_x64.dmg`

Installed copies update themselves from here (Settings › About).

**First launch:** Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click *More info* › *Run anyway*. On a Mac, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click *Open Anyway* once. Full steps in the README.
